Where the demo's data and claims come from

Data in the demo

Grid & energy · U.S. EIA Open Data, API v2: hourly demand and fuel mix for PacifiCorp East (balancing area PACE, Utah). Carbon intensity is our estimate from the generation mix. Prices: Rocky Mountain Power Utah Schedule 1 time-of-use tariff, effective Aug 10, 2026.

Health · the founder's own Samsung Health export (Galaxy Watch4), imported with consent and stored on the phone.

Smart home · the founder's Google Home device list (owner-provided). The agent reads it; it never switches devices.

Built on · Moto G 5G (2023), Android 13, rooted with Magisk.

Models

OpenAI Codex (gpt-6.1-terra) and GPT Live voice, through the founder's ChatGPT subscription. The auto-reviewer is the same model Codex uses for its auto mode.

Claude, through Claude Code.

On device · Qwen3.5-0.8B on llama.cpp (llama-server), no network.

Meta Llama Prompt Guard 2 (22M), on device through ONNX Runtime. Llama license.

Grok (xAI) reaches the phone over MCP through our relay; it can ask, only the phone approves.

Research behind the claims

Today's flagship phone agent: partner apps only, cloud-only: 9to5Google, Mar 12, 2026.reported

"AI must be human-guided": Gov. Cox, Utah Pro-Human AI principles (prohumanai.utah.gov).

Frameworks we follow: Meta's Rule of Two (Oct 2025) and DeepMind's CaMeL (arXiv 2503.18813).
