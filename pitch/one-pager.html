AgentOS · one-page plan · Nucleus Institute × Startup State open call · Oct 28, 2026 · draft

The Siri we should have. An AI-native phone OS, built security-first.

What it is. You say it, agents do it in your real apps. The model proposes; a gate in the OS decides; risky steps wait for a tap or your fingerprint; payments and passwords always require a fingerprint; everything is logged in a hash chain.

Where it stands. Working on a rooted Moto G 5G (2023), Android 13, our lab phone. Before this week: an island overlay prototype, SystemUI experiments, Codex running on the phone. Built Oct 26 to 28, 123 commits: the gate (leases, Rule of Two, on-device Prompt Guard 2, fingerprint-gated approvals, audit chain, per-app kernel firewall); agentd with Codex, Claude or an on-device model; cross-app memory; proactive cards; scheduled tasks on a locked phone; automatic offline fallback; live voice; an AI keyboard; app mods and micro-apps; the launcher.

Company. Utah-based founder, Utah LLC already formed: eligible now for UTIF microgrants, SBIR/STTR with Nucleus Grow, and the Nucleus Fund. 90-day goal. From one rooted lab phone to a shippable build, a 10-person Utah pilot, and an SBIR Phase I proposal ready to submit.
